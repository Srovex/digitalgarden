**Hitch**
They/Them

![Hitch.png](/img/user/40-49%20Extras/Files/Hitch.png)
### Appearance
- Skykin.
- 36 years old.
- Pale white, blue toned skin and hair.
- Intense eye makeup in contrast to the almost soulless blank eyes.
- Usually dresses in long trench coats in dark colors and loose fitting pants.

### Background

- Master’s degree in communications.
- Met with [[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Freddie\|Freddie]] during their time in the university.
- Has a strong network of underground contacts.
- Encouraged [[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Freddie\|Freddie]] to develop experimental brews and adrenaline shots for underground ring fighters, seeing potential in the thrill and the connections.
- After [[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Freddie\|Freddie]] opened up his shop, Hitch was there to quickly reconnect and strike a deal with the bat to work as his fixer for the assassination gigs he would perform.

### Personality

- Speaks in a slow manner and often stares at others uncomfortably long without blinking.
- Uses their communications background to study psychological tension during fights and deals.
- Takes quiet pride in pulling strings while pretending they’re merely “connecting people.”
- Incredibly efficient and dependable, has never betrayed Freddie’s trust.
