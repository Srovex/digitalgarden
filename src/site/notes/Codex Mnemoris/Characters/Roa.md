## Origin

Born as a human, tuned into a mouse. For reason unknow she was given at an young age to [[Codex Mnemoris/Factions/Bloom Index\|Bloom Index]] black-lab to be a test subject. She was never given a name - her designation was test subject R04. While in captivity she made a friend who was also a test subject. Her designation was L14 so they came up with names Roa and [[Lia\|Lia]].

Her body was modified to be more like mouse so they test results would be easier to get. [[Codex Mnemoris/Factions/Bloom Index\|Bloom index]] tested new drugs to make her body more durable and Roa’s noticed that her body was incredibly flexible. Roa lived like a test rat in cages and was treated like she didn’t matter, her only purpose was give answers.

One night the Roa wake up in to realize that the lab was on fire and the cell doors opened automatically. In panic Roa managed to squeeze through a crack in the wall and got out Unfortunately she couldn’t get her friend through so she had to escape alone. Outside was dark and scary so Roa hide for days. She didn’t want to go back to being tortured and live in tiny space so she decided to run away. Roa didn’t know how to speak or write properly so she just lived stealing food and listening to others. Little by little she learned how to talk more and started to speak with people. Roa was always careful not to get caught or raise suspicions so she ended up living in a forest.

One night different looking creature ended up wandering in her forest and Roa decided to approach them. Roa determined that this bat looking creature was stupid as it didn’t even know where to look for specific plants that were poisonous. Roa helped the bat person - [[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Freddie\|Freddie]].

[[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Freddie\|Freddie]] and Roa made a deal that Roa will help him with the plants and around the shop and in exchange she could live in [[Hemlock & Echo\|Hemlock & Echo]]. Freddie was nice enough to let her live with him.
