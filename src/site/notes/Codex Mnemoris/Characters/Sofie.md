### Background

- Human, around 30.
- Born and raised in the city.
- Works as a courier, transporting small packages and items, sometimes legal, sometimes not.
- Owns a motorcycle.
- She’s constantly on the move, which explains why she isn’t always around.
- Independent, street-smart, and good at navigating the city.
### Appearance

- Medium height, slim
- blond hair. brown eyes
- Usually wears fitted jeans, a black leather jacket, loose t-shirts, and sneakers.
- When partying, she goes all in on glitter, heels, and sparkly dresses.
- Several earrings and a few small tattoos.
### Personality

- Talkative, fearless, and quick to laugh.
- Says what’s on her mind — which makes her magnetic to some, irritating to others.
- Playful, flirtatious, and direct.
- Keeps things simple: she doesn’t promise more than she can deliver.
- Loves bars, music and parties.
- Easy with people and usually finds something to talk about with almost anyone.
### Connections to PCs:

#### [[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Nyméra\|Nyméra]]

- They have not had "the talk" and do not call each other girlfriends, partners, or anything similarly official. Have been together around 2 years.
- But they are clearly exclusive, private, unlabeled relationship.
- Nymera occasionally uses her as a courier between herself and Freddie, but Sofie is not part of the gym’s business and does not work there.
- Nymera tries to keep her out of her troubles, and Sofie does not know the full depth of Nymera’s business/deals/troubles.
##### How They met

- Sofie was already working as a courier long before their paths crossed. 
- They first met when one of her deliveries happened to be bound for Nyméra’s gym. 
- The flirting started on the spot, and after a few more runs their casual friendship fell into place. 
- Sofie made the first move with her characteristic open flirting. 
- Nyméra didn’t shut her down, and one night together turned into an occasional, recurring thing. 
#### [[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Bane\|Bane]]

- Friends who met at a bar.
- They party together and get along well.
#### Others

- Sofie likes the whole party and gets along easily with most people.
- She does not keep secrets that well and she loves rumors, gossip and ranting when she is frustrated.
