### How she met Nyméra

- Sofie was already working as a courier long before their paths crossed. 
- They first met when one of her deliveries happened to be bound for Nyméra’s gym. 
- The flirting started on the spot, and after a few more runs their casual friendship fell into place. 
- Sofie made the first move with her characteristic open flirting. 
- Nyméra didn’t shut her down, and one night together turned into an occasional, recurring thing. 

### How long they’ve known each other

- Two years — long enough for comfort and familiarity, not long enough to feel tied down. 

### Nature of the Relationship

- **Friends with benefits.** Clear boundaries: no promises, no commitment, no drama. 
- Sofie brings lightness and ease, giving Nyméra a chance to step outside her hardened role. 
- Nyméra gives Sofie honesty and a sense of safety, she never has to pretend. 

### How it looks to others
- Sofie occasionally shows up: picking Nyméra up after a night at the hall, or seen together in bars. 
- Their interactions are playful, touches, enough for others to recognize something is there. 
- Sofie adds a splash of warmth and humanity against Nyméras cold, controlled exterior.
