**Theta** is a mysterious woman [[Codex Mnemoris/Characters/Jax Vindle\|Jax]] met in the [[Codex Mnemoris/Factions/Bloom Index\|Bloom Index's]] floating laboratory ship [[The Water Lily\|The Water Lily]]. During the months they have known, Jax learned only a little about the girl except that she was held there by the Bloom operatives just like him. 

**Appearance:**

- Human, around 25.
- Extremely white & flawless skin, long platina blond hair
- 165cm / 55kg
- Grey-blueish eyes. When your eyes meet her gaze feels piercing.
- Her movements are calculated and delicate
- Currently wearing hospital gown like white dress which seem sizes too big for her
- No shoes or socks
