**Mother Vexline** is a small, dried woman with eyes like dark ponds and a raspy voice that carries authority on the crowded alleys of [[70-79 Atlantis/72 Locations/Gutterspire\|Gutterspires]]. No one knows her age; she simply _is_. In the district’s tangled streets she settles disputes, practices medicine, and opens her door to the desperate. Her home smells of broth, herbs, and wet stone.

When Jax arrived,she gave him a bed, a meal, and a warning: “Safety is borrowed. Respect is earned.”
